challenges.mp3: The road was not easy. The team faced five real challenges. Videos shorter than their audio: one command repeats the last frame, and we kept the originals. A lost message made the turntable over-rotate: we found it in the logs, and added a watchdog that stops it after one second with no message. Reversed pupil indices: we printed the raw coordinates, moved our eyes, and found the order was the opposite of common sources. Very quiet audio: we raised it in three places: the sound card, the system, and the player. A forgotten Raspberry Pi password: we booted it in a repair mode from the SD card and set a new password, without losing a single file.
